# Third-party data notices

WeightCoach can use the following external food-data sources. These notices
apply to the data returned by those services, not to WeightCoach's MIT-licensed
source code.

## USDA FoodData Central

The built-in common-food catalog includes values derived from USDA FoodData
Central. USDA states that FoodData Central data are in the public domain under
CC0 and asks applications to identify FoodData Central as the source.

- Source: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
- API and licensing guidance: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/

Individual catalog entries retain their FoodData Central identifiers so their
source can be checked.

## Open Food Facts

Barcode lookup can retrieve product records and images from Open Food Facts.
Open Food Facts publishes its database under the Open Database License (ODbL),
individual database contents under the Database Contents License, and product
images under CC BY-SA.

- Source: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/
- License guidance:
  https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/

The barcode result screen identifies Open Food Facts when it is the source.
If you redistribute a derived database or product images, review and satisfy
the applicable attribution, share-alike, and database-license requirements.
